OUR STORY

Powering Projects. Energizing Progress.

Rukn Al Hasan Electric Ware & Trade is a leading supplier of high-quality electrical materials based in Dubai, UAE. Specializing in wires, cables, energy solutions, and electrical accessories, we serve a wide range of clients — from individual buyers to large-scale contractors and corporate partners — across both retail and wholesale markets. With a foundation built on trust, performance, and affordability, we’ve proudly become a reliable partner for projects in residential, commercial, and industrial sectors throughout the Emirates.

Rukn Al Hasan Electric Ware & Trade is a Limited Liability Company incorporated in the United Arab Emirates. Located in Al Ghuwais Market, Sharjah, we trade in all electrical materials, sanitary products, hardware, tools, and air conditioning systems. By offering complete project solutions from a single source, we relieve project managers of logistical headaches—ensuring quality, timing, and compliance every step of the way. Through personalized service and unwavering commitment to customer needs, we’ve become the supplier of choice for bulk requirements and critical site shortages across the UAE.
